# The icon is not done

Everything in `res/drawable/ic_launcher_*.xml` is a placeholder: two rectangles and
two lines, drawn so that the app installs, appears on the launcher, and can be
photographed for the screenshots. It is not a design and it is not meant to survive.

The icons in this shop are traced hand drawings. The rules they follow are written
down and measured in `kinokocho/tools/make-art.py`. This one has not been near them.

**Do not publish a release with this icon in it.**

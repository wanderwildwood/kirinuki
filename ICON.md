# The icon

切抜 — a column cut out of a page with scissors. It lives in
`app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`.

Drawn to the five rules measured off the owner's chanterelle, which are written down
in `kinokocho/tools/make-art.py`: thin, one weight, few lines, crooked, no black.
Measured against that drawing rather than eyeballed — the chanterelle's pen line is
0.0143 of the width of what it draws, and this is 0.0150. Seven strokes to its eight.

Three approaches were tried and abandoned first, which is worth knowing before anyone
changes it: a zigzag cut edge reads as pinking shears; bowed edges make it a banner
rather than a flat cutting; and running the lines of type off the cut edge — the most
cutting-like move available, and correct in principle — fails at this weight, because
a text line meeting the outline becomes a crossbar and the drawing turns into a ladder.

⚠ **It is drawn rather than traced.** The other good icons here were traced from the
real thing, and the reason that matters is that every irregularity in this one was
invented, and so is regular in ways that do not show from the inside. Replacing it with
a trace of a real cutting — cut with real scissors, photographed flat — would be a
straight improvement, and the composition is settled, so only the hand is missing.
